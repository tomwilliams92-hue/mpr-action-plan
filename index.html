Where the time is going

This is a direct read-out of the admin load described in your own briefing — nothing here is guessed. Four processes currently rely on manual, repetitive staff time:

  • 1Compliance gathering & storage. Right-to-work checks, CSCS and share codes arrive over WhatsApp; a folder is created and filed by hand for every worker — ~4 hrs/week.
  • 2Weekly timesheets. 170 workers text their hours in; 3–4 staff manually populate and send timesheets across 65 sites for client sign-off every Monday — 9–12 staff-hours/week.
  • 3Mass communication. Messages to 70–80 people at a time are typed out manually from a phone tethered to a laptop, for chasing hours, compliance and job alerts.
  • 4Sourcing & onboarding admin. Job ads are written and posted manually to CV-Library; applicants are sifted and chased individually to get compliance documents started.

The action plan

Ordered by cost-effectiveness — cheapest and fastest to stand up first, structural change last.

⚙

Build it, don't rent it

Most of these tools don't need a monthly software subscription. The messaging dashboard, the compliance intake and the timesheet system can all be custom-built and owned outright — you pay a one-off to build them, then only minimal hosting (typically £0–£10/month). Where a genuine third-party cost remains it's flagged honestly: the SMS/WhatsApp carrier charges per message (pennies), and multi-jobboard posting still relies on the boards themselves. Items marked Owned build replace a recurring fee with software you keep.

In summary — the recommendation

If there are three things to take from this document, take these — ordered by what to do first.

Do first — this month
  • Build the three core tools that need no subscription: the compliance intake, the weekly-timesheet system (the single biggest time-saver), and the bulk-messaging dashboard. These are custom-built and owned outright — no recurring licence, just minimal hosting.
In parallel
  • Ask Talisman's vendor one question — is there an API? If yes, push the intake straight into the CRM and drop the shared-drive double-handling.
  • Trial a light off-the-shelf ATS for sourcing — the one area where paying a subscription is the honest, better-value call.
Later — once proven
  • Only then weigh up a single consolidated workforce platform to replace the patchwork outright, rather than automating around it indefinitely.
